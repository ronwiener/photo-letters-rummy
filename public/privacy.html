Privacy Policy for Photo Letter Word Wars

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

1. Overview

Photo Letter Word Wars is designed as a standalone game. We respect your privacy and believe in absolute transparency: we do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. You can download the app and start playing immediately without creating an account or providing any personal information.

2. Local Device Storage

All gameplay statistics, high scores, card settings, and game progress are stored exclusively on your local device (using local device storage). This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to or stored on any external cloud servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

We do not use third-party tracking, advertising networks, or user-tracking analytics inside Photo Letter Word Wars.

4. Data Control & Deletion

Since all game progress and statistics are saved locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can clear your game history and saved scores at any time by clearing the app data in settings or by uninstalling the application from your device.
